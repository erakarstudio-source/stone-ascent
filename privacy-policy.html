Privacy Policy for Stone Ascent

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stone Ascent ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you download and play the App. Stone Ascent is developed by erakarstudio ("the Developer"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Stone Ascent does not require you to create an account, and does not ask you to submit your name, email address, or any other personal contact information to play the game. The information handled by the App falls into the following categories:

a) Data stored only on your device

Your game progress — score history, Sacred Crystal and Heart balances, unlocked skins and biomes, mission/XP progress, and settings (language, sound, music, haptics, colorblind mode) — is stored locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. This data is never transmitted to, or stored on, any server operated by the Developer. If you uninstall the App or clear its storage, this data is permanently deleted.

b) Data collected by third-party services

The App uses the third-party services listed in the table below. These services may automatically collect certain information (such as your advertising identifier or device information) as described in their own privacy policies, linked below.

2. Third-Party Services

Service Purpose Data involved
Google AdMob Shows in-app advertising (rewarded video ads and interstitial ads) Advertising ID, device/app information, and usage data used for ad delivery and, where applicable, ad personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's User Consent Policy.
Google Play Billing (via RevenueCat) Processes in-app purchases (removing ads, extra Hearts, extra Sacred Crystal) Purchase and subscription-entitlement data associated with an anonymous app-generated user identifier, handled by RevenueCat on the Developer's behalf. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Google Play's policies.

We do not use any analytics SDK, and Stone Ascent does not include an online leaderboard, account system, chat, or any other feature that lets players communicate with each other or with us through the App.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To display and measure advertising, and (where you have not opted out) to personalize ads, via Google AdMob.
  • To process and validate in-app purchases and keep your purchased entitlements (such as "Remove Ads") in effect, via Google Play Billing and RevenueCat.
  • To remember your own game progress and settings, stored locally on your device, so the game works the same way each time you open it.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share any information with third parties except the service providers named above, to the extent needed for them to provide the functionality described.

4. Children's Privacy

Stone Ascent is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App targets a general audience. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • Local data: you can erase all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App from your device.
  • Ad personalization: you can reset or limit your device's advertising identifier, and manage ad personalization, through your device's system settings (Google Settings → Ads).
  • Data requests: if you would like to ask what information a third-party service listed above holds about you, or request its deletion, you can contact that service directly using the links above, or contact us and we will help direct your request.

6. Data Security

Because gameplay data is stored locally on your device rather than on a server we operate, it is protected by your device's own security. Data handled by our third-party service providers (Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, RevenueCat) is protected under their respective security practices, described in the policies linked above.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the App adds a new feature or third-party service. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continuing to use the App after a change takes effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
