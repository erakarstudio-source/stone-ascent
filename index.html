Privacy Policy for Stone Ascent

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stone Ascent ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you download and play the App. Stone Ascent is developed by erakarstudio ("the Developer"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Stone Ascent does not require you to create an account, and does not ask you to submit your name, email address, or any other personal contact information to play the game.

a) Data stored only on your device

Your game progress (score history, Sacred Crystal and Heart balances, unlocked skins and biomes, mission/XP progress) and settings (language, sound, music, haptics, colorblind mode) are stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to, or stored on, any server operated by the Developer. If you uninstall the App or clear its storage, this data is permanently deleted.

b) Data collected by third-party services

The App uses the third-party services listed below. These services may automatically collect certain information (such as your advertising identifier or device information) as described in their own privacy policies.

3. How Information Is Used

  • To display and measure advertising, and (where you have not opted out) to personalize ads, via Google AdMob.
  • To process and validate in-app purchases and keep your purchased entitlements (such as "Remove Ads") in effect.
  • To remember your own game progress and settings, stored locally on your device.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share any information with third parties except the service providers named above.

4. Children's Privacy

Stone Ascent is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

5. Your Choices and Rights

  • Local data: you can erase all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.
  • Ad personalization: you can reset or limit your advertising identifier and manage ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Data requests: to ask what information a third-party service listed above holds about you, or to request its deletion, contact that service directly or contact us and we will help direct your request.

6. Data Security

Gameplay data is stored locally on your device and protected by your device's own security. Data handled by our third-party providers (Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, RevenueCat) is protected under their respective security practices.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
